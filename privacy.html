Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10 · Applies to the HelmTV app for Android TV, version 0.1.0, and to this website.

In short: HelmTV has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it sends nothing to the HelmTV developer. Everything the app stores stays on your device. The app only connects to the provider you configure yourself.

1. Who is responsible

“The HelmTV developer” is the individual who makes the HelmTV app and this website. Contact: support@helmtv.app.

HelmTV is a media player. It does not provide, host, sell or link to any TV channels, films or series. You add your own Xtream-compatible provider (a name, a server address, a username and a password) the first time you run the app.

2. What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no user accounts, contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and does not send any data to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. The developer does not receive your provider details, viewing history or favourites, and cannot see them.

3. What is stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in its private app storage:

DataPurpose
Provider connection: a display name, server address, username and passwordTo log in to your provider. The address, username and password are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore before they are saved.
A cached copy of your provider's catalogue: categories, channels, films, series and programme guide dataSo browsing, search and the guide work quickly. It is refreshed from your provider when you open the app.
Favourites and watch progress (what you watched and how far)Favourites and “continue watching”.
Preferences: for example last watched channel, recently used and hidden categories, grouping, and keyboard layoutTo remember how you like the app set up.

The app does not keep a history of what you search for.

4. Network connections

The app uses the Android internet permission and the permission to check network state. It makes network requests only for these purposes:

  • Your provider. The app contacts the server address you entered to log in, download the catalogue and programme guide, and play streams. Your username and password are sent to your provider as part of these requests, as the Xtream protocol requires. A provider may redirect a stream to another server of its own, such as a content delivery host.
  • Images. Posters and channel logos are loaded from the image addresses that your provider's catalogue contains. Those addresses can point to servers that are not run by your provider, and such a server can see your IP address and which image was requested.

The app makes no other network requests. What your provider and any image server do with the information they receive is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Plain HTTP connections

Many providers only offer plain HTTP, and some of their streams are served over HTTP from other hosts. To make those providers work, the app allows unencrypted HTTP connections. If you enter a server address without http:// or https://, the app assumes http://. Over plain HTTP, other parties on the network path, such as your network operator, can potentially see your provider credentials and what you watch. Using HTTPS, where your provider supports it, and choosing a provider and network you trust is your responsibility.

5. Backups

The app allows Android's standard system backup. Depending on your device and Google account settings, Android may include app data in a backup under Google's own terms and privacy policy. Provider credentials are encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. Depending on your device, a restored backup may therefore not include working credentials, and you may need to enter your provider details again.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored on your device. You can also clear the app's storage in Android's settings. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to delete on the developer's side.

7. Google Play

If you install HelmTV through Google Play, Google may collect standard installation, usage and crash information (for example in Android vitals) under Google's own privacy policy. The developer sees only aggregated statistics that Google makes available in its developer console.

8. Children

HelmTV is not directed at children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. The app ships no content of its own; what you can watch is determined entirely by the provider you choose.

9. This website

This website is static. It uses no cookies, analytics, advertising, fonts or scripts from third parties. Its hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP addresses) as part of operating the service, under its own policy. If you email the developer, your message and address are used only to reply.

10. Your rights

The developer does not process personal data about you through the app, so there is generally nothing to access, correct or erase. If you believe otherwise, or have any question about this policy, write to support@helmtv.app.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date at the top.
