HelmTV — a player for your own IPTV on Android TV

A fast, remote-friendly app for watching live TV, films and series from the Xtream-compatible provider you already subscribe to.

HelmTV does not provide, host, sell or link to any TV channels, films or series. You bring your own subscription from a provider you are legally allowed to use.

Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Live TVChannels grouped by country and category, with quick zapping.
  • EPG guideA programme guide so you can see what is on now and next.
  • Movies and seriesBrowse your provider's film and series library.
  • SearchFind channels, films and series across your catalogue.
  • FavouritesKeep the channels and titles you watch most close at hand.
  • Continue watchingPick up films and episodes where you left off.
  • Subtitles and audioChoose subtitle and audio tracks while playing.
  • English and SwedishThe interface is available in both languages.

How it works

On first run you enter the details your provider gave you: a name, the server address, your username and your password. HelmTV then fetches your channel list and programme guide from that provider and plays the streams it offers. Nothing else is configured and no HelmTV account is needed.

HelmTV has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Read the privacy policy for the details.

About

Version 0.1.0. HelmTV is a personal project, designed for Android TV with a remote control.

It is built with open-source software, including:

  • Media3 / ExoPlayer for playback
  • Jetpack Compose, including Compose for TV, for the interface
  • Room for the local catalogue database
  • Paging for long lists
  • Coil for loading images
  • OkHttp for network requests
  • Jetpack DataStore for settings
  • The Figtree typeface (SIL Open Font License)

Content and copyright

HelmTV is a media player. It does not include, host, stream, sell or link to any TV channels, films, series or other content, and it has no built-in playlists. Everything you watch comes from a provider that you choose and that you are legally allowed to use.

Because HelmTV does not host any content, it cannot remove it. If you believe content is being distributed without permission, please contact the provider or host that serves it. Questions about the app itself go to support@helmtv.app.
