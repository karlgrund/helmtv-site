Your TV. Your provider.

A fast, remote-friendly player for the IPTV provider you already have.

See how it works

For Android TV and Chromecast with Google TV. Built for the remote.

HelmTV includes no channels, movies or series of its own. You need your own provider, and you add one you already have the right to use.

How it works

  1. Install HelmTVOn your Android TV or Chromecast with Google TV.
  2. Enter your provider's detailsThe name, server address, username and password your provider gave you.
  3. Pick categories and watchChoose what to show, then start watching. Nothing else to set up.

Features

  • Live TVBrowse by category and zap quickly with the remote.
  • Program guideSee what is on now and next, per channel.
  • Movies and seriesYour provider's library, with seasons and episodes.
  • SearchAcross live, movies and series. Recent searches stay on the device.
  • Favorites and continue watchingPick up where you left off.

Made for the remote

Every screen works with the arrow keys, OK and Back. Move with the arrows, press OK to open or play, press Back to go up one step. Press and hold OK on an item for more choices.

No touch, no mouse, no keyboard needed.

Questions

Does HelmTV include channels, movies or series?

No. HelmTV is a player. It includes no channels, movies or series of its own, and it does not sell any. You add the provider you already have the right to use.

Which providers work?

Providers that offer an Xtream-compatible login: a server address, a username and a password. If your provider gave you those three things, HelmTV can use them.

Do I need an account with you?

No. There is no HelmTV account. Your provider details are stored encrypted on your device.

Does it work on Chromecast with Google TV and Fire TV?

HelmTV is built for Android TV, including Chromecast with Google TV. Fire TV is not officially supported.

Can I use it on my phone?

No. HelmTV is made for Android TV and the TV remote.

I have no provider yet. Can I still use it?

Not on its own. HelmTV is only a player and includes no channels, movies or series, so it needs an Xtream-compatible service from a provider you choose. HelmTV does not sell or recommend any provider.

How do I get support?

Write to support@helmtv.app. HelmTV cannot help with your subscription or your provider's service. For those, contact your provider.

About

HelmTV is designed for Android TV with a remote control. It is built with open-source software, including:

  • Media3 / ExoPlayer for playback
  • Jetpack Compose, including Compose for TV, for the interface
  • Room for the local catalog database
  • Paging for long lists
  • Coil for loading images
  • OkHttp for network requests
  • Jetpack DataStore for settings
  • ZXing for the feedback QR code
  • The Figtree typeface (SIL Open Font License)

Content and copyright

HelmTV is a media player. It includes no TV channels, movies or series of its own, and it does not sell any. Everything you watch comes from a provider that you choose and that you are legally allowed to use.

Because HelmTV does not host or provide anyone else’s content, it cannot remove it. If you believe content is being distributed without permission, please contact the provider or host that serves it. Questions about the app itself, and copyright notices, go to support@helmtv.app.
